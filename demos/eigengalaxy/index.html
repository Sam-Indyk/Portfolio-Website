EigenGalaxy Explorer

Every galaxy image below is a vector of a few thousand pixels. PCA finds the handful of directions, the eigengalaxies, along which those vectors vary the most. Any galaxy can then be approximated as the mean galaxy plus a weighted sum of the first k of them, and that holds even if you blur it first.

How it works

Stack M centred galaxy images as rows of X (M × pixels). The eigengalaxies are the top eigenvectors of the covariance XᵀX. On thousands of images that matrix won't fit comfortably in memory at once, which is why EigenGalactic uses incremental PCA: it updates the basis one batch of 100 galaxies at a time.

Reconstruction is just projection: cᵢ = uᵢ · (x − μ) and x̂ = μ + Σᵢ₌₁ᵏ cᵢ uᵢ. A blurred galaxy projects onto the same basis, and because the basis only contains galaxy-like structure, the projection pulls the image back toward something galaxy-shaped.

The real basis couldn't be loaded, so this page trained its own: 600 procedurally generated galaxies (Sérsic ellipticals and log-spiral discs) at 48×48, eigen-decomposed through the Gram matrix XXᵀ with subspace iteration in about a second. The full EigenGalactic pipeline fits IncrementalPCA on Galaxy Zoo 2.
